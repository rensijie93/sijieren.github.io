About

I am a historian of science, technology, and medicine in modern China. My work centers on the politics, memory, and emotional life of scientific practice under Maoism, using China's synthetic insulin project as a central case for thinking about how science, the state, and revolutionary subjectivity were made together.

I hold a PhD in History from the University of Bristol, where my dissertation on the synthetic insulin project won the British Association for Chinese Studies (BACS) Best Doctoral Thesis Award. I am currently a Boya Postdoctoral Fellow at Peking University, supervised by Zhang Li (张藜), and remain an Honorary Research Associate at Bristol.
